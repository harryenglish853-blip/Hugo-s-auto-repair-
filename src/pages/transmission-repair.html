Don't wait on transmission symptoms

The transmission sends the engine's power to the wheels. When it isn't working the way it should, the vehicle may not accelerate, shift or hold a gear reliably. Getting it looked at early is safer than waiting to see if it gets worse.

Hugo's handles transmission repair and transmission service, often alongside engine and A/C work on the same vehicle.

What to tell the shop

  • Your vehicle's year, make and model, and whether it's an automatic or manual.
  • When the symptom happens: cold starts, after warming up, at certain speeds, or going uphill.
  • Any warning lights, and any fluid you've noticed under the vehicle.

Symptoms worth a call

  • Engine revs up but the vehicle doesn't accelerate as expected (slipping)
  • Harsh, delayed or unusually early shifts
  • A delay before the vehicle moves after shifting into drive or reverse
  • Whining, humming or clunking that changes with gear or speed
  • Reddish fluid under the vehicle, or a burning smell

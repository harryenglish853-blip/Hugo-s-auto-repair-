Something doesn't feel right?

You don't need to know what's wrong to call. Tell Hugo's what you're seeing, hearing or feeling and when it happens: at startup, while braking, at highway speed, when turning. Those details help the shop understand where to start.

What Hugo's works on

  • Engine repair and diagnostics, including motor replacement
  • Brakes and rotors
  • Suspension and steering, including control arms
  • Electrical repair
  • A/C repair and service
  • Oil changes and maintenance
  • Welding and fabrication

Before you call

  • Have your vehicle's year, make and model handy.
  • Note any warning lights on the dashboard.
  • If a warning light is flashing, or the vehicle is overheating, smoking or leaking heavily, pull over safely and call before driving further.

Common reasons drivers call

  • A warning light on the dashboard
  • A new noise: squealing, grinding, clunking or knocking
  • Fluid spots under the vehicle
  • An unusual smell, smoke or steam
  • A change in how the vehicle starts, idles, stops or drives

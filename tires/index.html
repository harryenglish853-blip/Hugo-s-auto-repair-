Why tire condition matters

Braking distance, cornering grip and how your vehicle handles in a sudden summer monsoon all depend on the condition of your tires. Worn tread, low pressure or damage you can't easily see can all affect how safely a vehicle stops and steers.

Phoenix heat and hot pavement are hard on rubber. Checking tire pressure and condition regularly is one of the simplest things you can do for your vehicle's safety.

Talk to Hugo's about tires

Call the shop to describe what you need: your tire size (printed on the sidewall), what you're noticing, and when you'd like to come in. Hugo's will let you know how they can help.

Recent work at the shop includes leveling kits paired with new all-terrain tires on full-size pickups.

Signs it may be time to have your tires checked

  • Tread looks low, or is worn more on one edge than the other
  • Cracks, bulges, cuts or bubbles in the sidewall
  • A tire keeps losing air, or the tire-pressure light comes on
  • Vibration through the steering wheel or seat at speed
  • A nail, screw or other object in the tread

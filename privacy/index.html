Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy explains how Hugo's Alignment, Tires & Auto Repair ("Hugo's," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website.

Information you give us

If you submit the service request form, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address (if provided), vehicle year, make and model, the service you're asking about, and your message. We use this information only to respond to your request and to provide the service you ask about.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability. This website does not use advertising cookies.

Third-party services

  • Maps. The embedded map and directions links are provided by Google Maps (or Apple Maps on Apple devices). Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies when you use them.
  • Form delivery. Service requests are delivered through a third-party form-processing service that transmits your submission to us.
  • Review and financing sites. Links to review sites such as Yelp, or to financing providers, take you to websites governed by their own policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate this website and respond to you, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep service request information only as long as needed to respond and for our normal business records.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete information you've submitted, call us at (602) 242-0442 or visit us at 6040 N Black Canyon Hwy, Phoenix, AZ 85017.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
